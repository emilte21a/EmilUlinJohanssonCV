Project Overview

This project was created as part of a course called "Mobile Application Development", where me and five others developed a mobile video game.

The course took place from mid-March 2025 until June 2025. The game was later released on Google Play.

Audio Clips

I made these sound effects using the Digital Audio Workstation FL Studio

losesound.mp3

This sound is played whenever the player has lost a fight-round against the enemy.

winsound.mp3

This sound is played whenever the player has won a fight-round against the enemy.

newbackgroundmusicmaybe.mp3

This music is played in the background during the chess-phase of the game.

newfightmusic.mp3

This music is played in the background during the fight-phase of the game.

transitionsfx.mp3

This sound is played whenever the game transitions from the chess-phase to the fight-phase.

powerup.mp3

This sound is played whenever either the player, or the enemy picks up a powerup during the chess-phase.
