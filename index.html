About Me

My name is Emil Ulin Johansson, and I am a game-design student at Stockholm University. Previously, I studied game development at NTI gymnasiet Odenplan which has given me years of experience in many areas of game development. Primarily I have been programming in C# which has taught me many programming principles as well as design architectures.

Additionally, I have produced music as a hobby since 2019 both in real life and digitally. Music is something I have had passion for forever, and that is why I especially adore well designed soundscapes and video games. Therefore I ensure that both sound design, and music-production is of the best quality.
