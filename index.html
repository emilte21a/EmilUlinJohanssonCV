My name is Emil Ulin Johansson, and I am a game-developer student at Stockholm University. Previously,, I studied Game Development at NTI Gymnasiet Odenplan, where I gained several years of experience across different areas of game development.

My primary programming experience is in C#, which has given me a strong understanding of programming principles, software architecture and the technical foundations of game development. During my studies I have also developed an interest in the relationship between technology, game-design and player experience.

Additionally, I have produced music as a hobby since 2019 both digitally and through live instruments. Music has always been a passion of mine which has led me to the importance of well-crafted sound design and atmosphere in games. I am especially interested in creating immersive soundscapes using music and sound design to strengthen a game's identity.

I enjoy exploring the intersection between game design, programming, music and audio, and I strive to create experiences where these elements coincide to strengthen the player's experience.
