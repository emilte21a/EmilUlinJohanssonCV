About Me

I'm a game development student at Stockholm University with a strong focus on becoming a gameplay programmer.

My name is Emil Ulin Johansson, and I am a game-developer student at Stockholm University. Previously, I studied Game Development at NTI Gymnasiet Odenplan, where I gained several years of experience across different areas of game development.

My primary programming language is C# which I have most experience in. During my time at NTI I learned how to use C# in Unity and Raylib. I also have experience in C++, Unreal Engine and SDL3, which has given me a great understanding of low-level programming, memory management and graphics frameworks.
