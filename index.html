My name is Emil Ulin Johansson, and I am a game-developer student at Stockholm University. Previously,, I studied Game Development at NTI Gymnasiet Odenplan, where I gained several years of experience across different areas of game development. My primary programming language is C# which I have most experience in. During my time at NTI I learned how to use C# in Unity and Raylib. I also have experience in C++, Unreal Engine and SDL3, which has given me a great understanding of low-level programming, memory management and graphics frameworks.

I take great pleasure in well-crafted gameplay mechanics, and I am especially interested in procedural-content-generation(PCG).

Fun Fact: I have produced music as a hobby since 2019 both digitally with FL Studio and through live instruments like drums and the piano.
